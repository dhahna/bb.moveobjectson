Sims 4 House Style Finder

50 American residential architecture styles, broken into the elements that define them — roofs, foundations, cladding, windows, ornament — with the Sims 4 packs and Build/Buy item types that get you there. Pick a preset, search by element, or browse by pack.

Made by @dhahna · assumes all packs, game packs, stuff packs, and kits are owned · each item needs its listed pack installed

How to Use It

  1. Stuck for an idea? Start in Style Presets and browse by era.
  2. Got one detail in mind? Use Feature Finder — search “gambrel” or tap facet chips and see which styles match.
  3. Ready to build? The style card lists item types by pack; Pack & Item Browser filters the same list by what you own.
  4. Hit a word you don’t know? It’s in the Glossary, with how to fake it in-game.

Style Presets

Fifty American house styles. Tap one to open its full card — roof, foundation, cladding, windows, ornament — plus the Build/Buy items that get you there. Every element on the card is tappable and jumps you into the Feature Finder.

Feature Finder

Type anything (e.g. gambrel, wraparound porch, board-and-batten, Cottage Living) and/or tap facet chips. Matching styles are ranked by how many of your picks they hit; matched elements are highlighted gold.

Pack & Item Browser

Every Build/Buy recommendation in this guide, flattened. Filter by pack, category, or style. Click a row to open the style.

Sourcing note: EA doesn't publish a machine-readable Build/Buy catalog, so this is a curated, pack-accurate list of item types per style (e.g. "Get Together → half-timbered walls") rather than a dump of every catalog object. In-game, filter Build/Buy by the listed pack and category to surface the exact swatches.
